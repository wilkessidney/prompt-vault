Build a webinar registration block with {{技术栈：Tailwind CSS / 原生 CSS / UnoCSS / styled-components}} as a wide bordered card split into two parts. The left part carries a small "Live session" pill with a red dot, a heading naming the session, a two-line description, a row of three detail items with glyph tiles for date, duration and format, and a line of speaker credits with two small overlapping avatars and their names and roles. The right part is a neutral-50 panel with a heading reading "Save your seat", an email field, a company field, a dark full-width register button, and under it a muted line saying the recording is sent to everyone who registers even if they cannot attend. Add a bottom bar across the whole card showing three past-session links with dates in monospace and a link to the full archive on the right.

Deliver the result as {{输出格式：单个 HTML 文件（内联 style 与 script） / React 组件（.tsx） / Vue 单文件组件（.vue） / Astro 组件（.astro）}}.
