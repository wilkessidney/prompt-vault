Design a social proof band with {{技术栈：Tailwind CSS / 原生 CSS / UnoCSS / styled-components}} combining review-site badges with one quote. Use a max-w-5xl bordered rounded-2xl card. Across the top, a row of four badge tiles separated by hairlines: each has the review site name in small uppercase, a row of five star glyphs with the last one half-toned via opacity, a rating figure like 4.8 out of 5, and a muted count of reviews. Below the badges, a centred quote area on a neutral-50 background: a large quotation mark glyph, two sentences of testimonial in medium-sized relaxed type, then an attribution line with an avatar, name, role and company on one row. Under the quote, three small award-style pills reading things like "Leader, Spring 2026" with a diamond glyph. Keep the whole composition calm — one accent colour at most, everything else neutral.
